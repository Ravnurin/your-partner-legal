Your Partner — Support

A private notebook about one person.

Log a detail in about five seconds, browse it back when you want to be thoughtful.

Get in touch

Email runibrockie@live.com. Replies usually come within two working days. It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

How do I sign in?
Tap Sign in with Apple on the first screen. There is no password to remember. If you chose to hide your email address, Apple forwards mail through a private relay and the app never sees your real address.

Where are my notes?
They live in your own account on the server and are cached on your phone so you can read them without a connection. Writing a new note needs a connection.

Can anyone else read them?
Only the one partner you link with, and only what you choose to share. Linking takes an invite code you give them. After that you can share dates, firsts and noticed notes one at a time. Your notes in the categories, your people and the profile are never shared. There is no public profile and no feed.

How do I stop sharing?
Turn sharing off on an item to hide it from your partner again. To stop sharing altogether, unlink in Settings: you lose access to what they shared straight away, and what you shared stays with them, read-only, until they unlink too. Delete anything you'd rather they didn't keep.

How do I delete my account?
Open the Settings tab and choose Delete account. That permanently removes your account, every note, every category, the profile, every date, first and person, and all their photos. Anything you shared disappears for your partner too. It cannot be undone. Deleted the app already? Reinstall it and sign in with the same Apple ID: you land back in the same notebook and can delete it from Settings. Email can only find your account if you shared your real address when you first signed in, so the app is the route that always works.

How do I change the categories?
Tap Edit on the home screen. From there you can rename, reorder, delete, and add your own.

Can I use it for more than one person?
Not today. The app is built around one notebook about one person.

I found a bug.
Email it. Include what you tapped and what happened.

Report a concern

If a partner shares something abusive or threatening with you, or you are worried about how someone is using the app, email runibrockie@live.com with the subject "Report a concern", or tap Report a concern in Settings. Say what was shared and when. Unlinking in Settings stops them seeing anything more of yours straight away.

Every report is read within 24 hours. Content that breaks the Terms of Use is deleted and the account that shared it is closed.

If you are in danger, call your local emergency number first.
