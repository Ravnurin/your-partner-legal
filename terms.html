The app

Your Partner is a private notebook about one person. You can link it with one partner's account and share dates, firsts and noticed notes with them. It is provided by Rúni Brockie, an individual developer, free of charge.

Your account

You must be at least 16 to use the app. Keep your Apple ID secure: anyone signed in to it can open your notebook. You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

What you write

What you write stays yours. You let the app store it, and show it to you and to the partner you share it with, so the app can work. That permission ends when you delete it.

No tolerance for abuse

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You must not use the app to share, or to store for sharing, anything that:

  • threatens, harasses, intimidates or demeans another person;
  • is hateful towards anyone for who they are;
  • shows or describes sexual content involving anyone under 18;
  • is an intimate image of someone shared without their consent;
  • is illegal where you or your partner live.

You must not use the app to monitor, control or pressure a partner.

Reporting and what happens next

If something shared with you breaks these terms, email runibrockie@live.com with the subject "Report a concern", or use Report a concern in Settings. Say what was shared and when. Unlinking from Settings stops that person seeing anything more of yours straight away, and works as a block.

Every report is read within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is deleted, and the account that shared it is closed.
